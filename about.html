My Story

Cleveland born and raised. Real estate wasn't something I chose at 22 after scrolling through an investing podcast. I was 10 years old helping my family flip houses. I saw what it looks like when a deal goes right. I saw what it looks like when it doesn't. I watched people lose money on properties they were convinced were slam dunks. I watched people break even for three years on something they thought would change their life. And I watched people build real wealth, slowly, carefully, because they actually understood what they were buying.

Before I got my license I was in wholesale, finding deals, running numbers, figuring out what a property is actually worth to someone putting real money into it. Not what Zillow says. Not what the seller hopes. What an investor would actually pay.

Now I'm a licensed Realtor focused on investment properties across Cuyahoga County. I own properties here too, so when we're looking at a deal together, I'm asking the same questions I ask myself.

If it's a bad deal, I'll tell you it's a bad deal.

If you're newer to this and you've got that anxious feeling, like you don't want to be the person who buys the wrong thing, I get it. I've seen that play out. I know what the mistakes actually look like. That's the whole point of this site: I tell you what I see, not what sounds good.
