Where should I Invest with my Hard Earned Money?

April 1, 2026

For Cuyahoga County, I’ve developed a simple framework to keep my decisions consistent and profitable:

Only buy in C/D neighborhoods for cash flow or BRRR opportunities.
These areas tend to offer lower purchase prices and stronger rent to price ratios. The play here is simple: buy right, stabilize, and either hold for income or recycle your capital. Appreciation is a bonus, not the reason for the deal.

B/A neighborhoods are rarely cash flow plays.
Higher purchase prices usually compress returns, making it difficult to hit meaningful monthly cash flow. Instead, these areas are better suited for flips or appreciation-based strategies where resale value and buyer demand drive profit.

At the end of the day, there’s no “best” place to invest, only what aligns with your strategy. If you go into a deal expecting cash flow in an appreciation market (or vice versa), you’ll miss your targets. Define your goal first, then choose the area that supports it.
